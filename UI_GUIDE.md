# Guided reception UI

The assistant follows automatically. The entry-choice screen and office C/L entry-selection shortcuts are removed.

The HUD has a small decision-points badge at the top right, a brief queued reward notification near the top, a crosshair/context prompt when needed, and an `i` button at the bottom left. Click `i` with the cursor released, or press I, to open/close the drawer containing the current/next task and control keys. Escape also closes the drawer. The first notification waits four seconds before appearing. Instruction cards fade in/out over half a second, with a short gap between consecutive cards. Close dismisses a card without completing its task or awarding points; the task remains in the drawer, and normal gameplay actions still work.

OfficeGuidance owns the decision gates: flashlight, careful desk approach, hands-up command, checking both identities, surrender, covering handcuffs, escorting both people, and taking the stairs. Cards pause game time and release the cursor until the player selects an action. Flashlight F works while its card is open. Normal room lighting stays visible from the first frame; only the player's torch starts off. The suspect's hands-up notification waits 3.5 seconds from the start of his stand-up blend (about three seconds after he stands). Other cards explain the next physical action, such as aiming and pressing Q; selecting Continue does not skip identification or surrender requirements.

First flashlight use awards 50 points, choosing to cover restraint 25, choosing to escort both people 50, and a justified kneel command uses the command reward (80). Existing identification, corner and restraint rewards remain. DecisionScore rejects duplicate IDs. MissionHud subscribes to reward events, queues at most six messages, and dismisses each toast automatically after 3.8 seconds, with fade-in/fade-out, using unscaled time. Zero-point bookkeeping messages do not occupy the HUD.

FieldUi provides shared rounded panels and buttons for the HUD and lift dialog using Unity's built-in rounded GUI drawing. GUI styles are cached; no generated textures, post-processing or third-party UI assets are required. UI scaling follows viewport height to preserve corner and font proportions. Layout, character placements, bake files and WebGL rendering settings are unchanged.

Active corner checks show a compact progress bar and distinguish the player's scan from an assistant scan. A completed scan shows a two-second confirmation and retains the existing one-time reward. Interrupted scans reset; another observer's progress is preserved. Shooting the unarmed suspect waits for the supplied Fall animation's completed state before showing "You shot an unarmed suspect. Mission failed." The penalty toast is held back during the animation too. Clearance > Test Death And Scan Feedback checks these failure/scanning paths and writes Logs/Office-feedback-test.txt.
