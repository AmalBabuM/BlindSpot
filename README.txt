BLINDSPOT - CLEARANCE BUILDING

START
Open the hosted WebGL game in a desktop browser. Select Start Game,
then watch or skip the intro. In Unity, open GroundFloor and press Play.

CONTROLS
WASD        Move
Mouse       Look / aim
Shift       Move carefully
F           Toggle flashlight
Q           Give a voice command to the person you are aiming at
E           Interact / open the stair door carefully / restrain nearby
1 / 2       Select stun device / firearm
Left click  Use the selected tool
I           Show instructions and controls
Esc         Pause, resume, or return to the main menu

HOW TO PLAY
1. Turn on your flashlight. Check corners and approach the reception desk.
2. When the suspect stands, aim at either person and press Q for hands up.
3. Keep your flashlight aimed steadily at each person from within 3.5 metres
   to identify the employee and suspect.
4. Aim at the suspect and press Q again to order him to kneel. Cover your
   assistant while he applies cuffs and escorts both people to the exit.
5. Wait for your assistant to return, then approach the stairs. Use E to
   open the stair door slowly. Pushing it open suddenly triggers an ambush.
6. Aim at the armed suspect and press Q to drop the gun and raise hands.
   Wait for him to comply, then press Q again to order surrender. Let your
   assistant apply cuffs to complete the mission.

Good decisions earn points. Protect the employee, verify before using
force, and avoid shooting unarmed or complying people. The final report
shows your score and recorded decisions. The lift is unsafe; use the stairs.

WEBGL BUILD NOTE
Serve the entire Builds/WebGL folder over HTTP/HTTPS. Do not open its
index.html directly from disk. Keep Build, TemplateData and StreamingAssets
alongside index.html.
